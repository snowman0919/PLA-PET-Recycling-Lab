## Material evidence before breakage optimization
For each of PLA/PET/TPU: grade/brand, lot, aging/recycling history, hardness for TPU, moisture/conditioning, print orientation/layer height/infill, initial flake maximum caliper/equivalent diameter/minimum thickness, mass-based distributions, sample size and repeats. Do not mix PETG with PET.

Test sequence: S1 feed distribution; low-energy single-edge cut force-displacement at controlled temperature; friction/relaxation and elastic rebound; repeat cycles including nonfracturing deformation; metal and outlet thermal step response; mechanically guarded low-load S2 run. Electrical/mechanical commissioning must be separately authorized and physically supervised.

Each record requires trace files, units and sampling rates, synchronized encoder and torque/current, coupon/fixture dimensions, material grade/lot, actuator/force-sensor calibration, independent calibration-validation IDs, CAD revision and hashes. Motor current alone is not a calibrated torque sensor without a tested motor/gear loss map.
